---

## The Problem (0:15 - 0:35)

Traditional escrow requires trusted middlemen.

Clients worry: "What if the provider never delivers?"

Providers worry: "What if the client refuses payment?"

Legal escrow is slow. Expensive. Requires lawyers.

---

## Our Solution (0:35 - 1:00)

VaultRail is a smart contract system on Solana.

Clients create an escrow. They set milestones.

Funds stay locked in the program.

Only the authorized client can approve a milestone.

Only released funds go to the provider.

---

## Key Features (1:00 - 1:20)

Non-custodial: We do not hold your funds.

Milestone-based: Release happens per step.

PDA security: Cryptographic accounts prevent fraud.

Full audit trail: Every action is recorded.

Client-controlled: You decide what gets released.
