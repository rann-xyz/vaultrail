## Recording Tips

1. **Browser:** Use Chrome or Edge, navigate to the live URL
2. **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (16:9) or 1280x720 minimum
3. **Frame rate:** 30fps for smooth interaction
4. **Cursor:** Show clear, deliberate clicks
5. **Audio:** Clean narration, no background noise
6. **File:** Export as MP4 for YouTube compatibility

## Honest Disclaimer (Required in Video)

> "This dashboard is a prototype. It is not connected to Solana, and on-chain transactions have not been demonstrated. All interactions are simulated locally in the browser."
